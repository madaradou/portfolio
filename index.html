01

Module 3 — shipped

First-person controller, pick-up / drop extinguishers with sway & recoil, hold-to-extinguish with sphere-cast + line-of-sight (no spraying through walls), one extinguisher per fire, NavMesh NPC that takes smoke damage, UI Toolkit menus and a one-click scene builder.

02

Module 4 — electrical fire

Fire stages that spread to the cable tray and re-ignite if the power is still on, a main-breaker power cut with emergency lighting, safety-decision scoring and a debrief, plus three difficulties: Facile, Normal, Examen.

03

The panicking coworker

An NPC runs at the fire with a water extinguisher. Stop him, hold [E] to calm him down, then guide him crouched through the smoke to the assembly point — with French voice lines, subtitles and heartbeat audio.

CASE STUDY 02 — SMART AGRICULTURE

ESS9INI إسقيني

A smart-farm dashboard built for Tunisian agriculture — first for an olive farm in Gabès (مزرعة الزياتين، قابس). IoT soil sensors stream moisture, temperature and battery levels; an AI layer turns soil + weather into irrigation recommendations, in an Arabic / English interface farmers can use in the field.

  • Real-time monitoring — 4 sensors, updates every 30 s, offline detection
  • AI irrigation — weather-aware schedules for olive trees, date palms and cereals
  • Interactive farm map — click a sensor, colour-coded by moisture
  • Alerts — critical < 30 %, warning 30–60 %, target 80 %

02 — DOSSIER TECHNIQUE AI · 26 OF 30 COMMITS

STEG dossiers in minutes

A full-stack platform that generates the official 21-page STEG technical dossier for a grid-connected PV installation. It handles sizing, NF C 15-100 checks and a ready-to-submit PDF with annexes and GPS data. I built an AI datasheet scanner that pulls panel, inverter, protection and cable specs out of PDFs. I also built a function-calling dossier agent (Qwen3-8B) that reads and edits the dossier, runs compliance and writes the French narrative text, falling back to a rule-based checklist when the AI is unavailable. On top of that I added ERP modules and an Expo mobile client.
